Why neighbors choose us

Three things that make a real difference

Dry cleaned on premises

Your clothes never leave the building. No shuttling to an off-site plant means less handling, fewer lost items, faster turnaround — and if something needs extra attention, we spot it and fix it ourselves.

Same-day service

Interview at four? Wedding this evening? Drop it off in the morning and pick it up the same day. Just let us know at the counter and we'll make it happen.

Family owned & operated

We're not a chain. The people who greet you are the people who own the shop and press your shirts — and we've built this business one neighbor at a time.
